# Changelog

## v6

- **Dreadnought.** Level 4 is now Orbital: a 2 km warship that warps in
  overhead. A point of heat grows in the sky, the sensor whites out, and the
  ship drops in trailing a hot wake and brakes to a stop broadside to you. It
  launches three craft from its belly hangars every 10 seconds. 50,000 HP,
  with the drives, bow emitter, bridge, hangars and radiator wings as weak
  points.
- **Capital ship wrecks.** The dreadnought falls into the sea burning when it
  dies, with explosions that follow it down.
- **Sensor whiteout** on warp arrivals and on the final blast.
- New warp arrival sound: a rising drone that ends in a crack and a long boom.
- Random waves now start at level 5.
- Big ship hit tests are much cheaper, which matters with 90 hit spheres.
- Weak points now line up with scaled models.

## v5

- **Gun smoke removed**, along with the wind readout and the bore gas bar.
- **Mothership.** A 360 m airship carrier. It closes to about 1.2 km, turns
  broadside and circles while its belly bay drops three drones or jets every
  8 seconds. 14,000 HP, with engine pods, the bay and the bridge as weak points.
- **Scripted opening levels.** Level 1 First Contact, Level 2 Saturation,
  Level 3 Mothership. The random wave director takes over from Level 4.
- **Title screen** with controls and best score.
- **Level banners**, plus a warning with bearing when a boss arrives.
- **Hit markers** on every hit, a bigger one on kills, and a SPLASH kill
  confirmation under the cross.
- **Boss integrity bar** with range and bearing.
- **Big ship hitboxes** built from several spheres, so you can't hit a giant
  ship by shooting the empty sky beside it. Missiles fuze on the hull too.
- **Damage fires** on big ships at 75%, 50% and 25%, placed where you hit.
- **Death sequence** for big ships: a ripple of explosions down the hull.
- Muzzle flash now flickers instead of showing as a solid square.
- Deck surfaces no longer speckle.

## v4

- Gun sound rebuilt to be heavier and less muffled.
- Lock beep now uses the same tone as the lock-on sound.
- Reload shortened to 2.4 s.
- Two kinds of screen shake: a steady judder while the gun runs, and a short
  hard kick on missile launch.

## v3

- Fixed tracers looking like horizontal bars when firing east or west.
- Lock takes about a second longer.
- Reload shortened to 3 s.

## v2

- Fixed mouse aim not working (input wasn't reaching the turret).
- Everything playable from the mouse.
- Lock on missile and salvo added.

## v1

- Thermal look, 360° turret, gun, enemy table, waves, HUD.
